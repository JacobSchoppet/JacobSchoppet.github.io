About LiftByDay

LiftByDay is a free fitness resource designed to help people of all experience levels find a workout split that fits their schedule. Whether you can only make it to the gym twice a week or you are ready to train six days a week, LiftByDay has a structured plan for you.

Our Mission

The mission of LiftByDay is simple: make structured workout planning accessible to everyone. Many people, especially beginners, struggle to organize their training around their busy lives. Without a clear plan, it is easy to skip workouts, waste time at the gym, or feel overwhelmed by the amount of information available online.

LiftByDay solves this by taking the guesswork out of programming. Instead of browsing through hundreds of different routines, you simply choose how many days per week you can train and we provide a proven split that works for your schedule and experience level.

Who Is LiftByDay For?

LiftByDay is built for anyone who wants to get more out of their time in the gym. Our primary audience includes:

  • Beginners who are just starting their fitness journey and need a clear, simple plan to follow.
  • Intermediate lifters who have some gym experience but want a more structured and effective training split.
  • Busy individuals such as college students and young professionals who need a plan that fits around a demanding schedule.
  • Anyone looking to be more consistent, build strength, and make real progress toward their fitness goals.

Fitness is for everyone regardless of background or budget. All of the content on LiftByDay is completely free.
